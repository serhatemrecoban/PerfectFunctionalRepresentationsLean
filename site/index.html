01 / THE QUESTION

How much information can the auxiliary carry?

A functional representation reconstructs an output using the input and independent auxiliary randomness. Perfectness asks whether that auxiliary attains the natural information bound.

Functional representation
X ⟂ Z,   Y = f(X, Z)The decoder equality is required on the support of the law.

The auxiliary Z is independent of X; together they determine Y.

Perfect representation
I(Y; Z) = H(Y | X)Finite value alphabets; information measured in nats.

Equality in the bound is characterized by the Markov property X → Y → Z.

The information results have explicit finite-alphabet and support contracts. Read the limitations.
