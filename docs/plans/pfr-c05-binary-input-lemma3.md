## 3. Mathematical contracts and hypothesis allocation

The following notation describes required statements, not new parallel
definitions of probability or information. All entropy/information is the
pinned Real-valued API in nats. Products `alpha × beta × gamma` associate to
the right. `X(t)=t.1`, `Y(t)=t.2.1`, `Z(t)=t.2.2` denote triple projections.

### 3.1 Binary coordinates and Equation (10)

For the numerical TV/overlap statements, assume `[Fintype beta]` and let
`kappa : Fin 2 → PMF beta`. Put, in this document only,

`r(kappa) = finiteTV (kappa 0) (kappa 1)`.

Do not duplicate TV or define a second mismatch probability. Equation (10)
must be connected to the existing half-L1 Real expression by unfolding or a
checked bridge, and to `1 - (overlapMass (kappa 0) (kappa 1)).toReal` by C04.
The paper's labels 1/2 correspond to Lean's 0/1.

Use mathlib's `finTwoArrowEquiv beta : (Fin 2 → beta) ≃ beta × beta`.
Mapping a product law through its inverse produces the vector law; mapping
back recovers the original law. Prove the equivalence

`IsCoupling (kappa 0) (kappa 1) g ↔
 IsCanonicalCoupling kappa (g.map (finTwoArrowEquiv beta).symm)`.

The product/vector and structural coupling-law equivalences do not require
finite beta; retain that generality. The `[Fintype beta]` assumption belongs
to the finite TV/overlap statements, not to these structural maps.

Supply the coordinate/evaluation law bridges needed to use both directions,
not just an equivalence of carriers. In particular the canonical triple has
the prescribed pair law for **every** input prior.

For arbitrary finite `alpha` and `e : alpha ≃ Fin 2`, use
`r_e(kappa) = finiteTV (kappa (e.symm 0)) (kappa (e.symm 1))`.
This is notation, not an additional public definition. Check row order and
prove independence of the enumeration e, using the two-element alternatives
and C04 symmetry. The semantic intrinsic contract uses
`Fintype.card alpha = 2` with a checked `Fintype.equivFinOfCardEq` transport.
Quantitative semantic wrappers may retain an explicit e, together with the
enumeration-independence theorem. No hidden cast changes a channel or prior.

### 3.2 Binary singularity and combined Lemma 2

For `[Fintype alpha] [Fintype beta] [Nonempty alpha]`,
`kappa : alpha → PMF beta` and `Fintype.card alpha = 2`, establish

`IsPerfectlyRepresentableChannel kappa ↔ IsSingularChannel kappa`.

The new direction is singularity sufficiency and is prior-free. Construct a
common-posterior witness using the binary maximal coupling and C02's existing
factorization contract. The witness must work on every channel input and
must allow unused output symbols.

The common posterior must be total, including both-zero output fibers,
without an additional Nonempty-beta premise or a new normalized zero-mass
distribution. The recommended construction uses the pinned total conditional
channel's first-marginal fallback, the vector law q. An alternative completion
at globally unused outputs is allowed with checked factorization preservation,
using C02's `coordinateFactorization_congr_posterior`; positive-output
factorizations and the required hypotheses must remain unchanged.

Retain the source meaning of (111)-(124): on shared positive outputs the two
row masses agree and the selected vector is constant; exclusive outputs
determine the input. Identify the shared mass in (115) with C04 overlap and
the singular specialization of C04's atom law with (121): diagonal shared
mass, plus the product of the two exclusive masses divided once by the
residual total. Handle overlap 0 and 1 through C04 endpoints. Separate
normalized `qA/qB/qC` definitions are not required, but this representation
bridge is required; it cannot be replaced by an unexplained appeal to a
different construction.

Expose a combined paper-facing Lemma 2 that supplies both generic necessity
and sufficiency when input cardinality is two **or** active-output cardinality
is at most two. Also provide a direct consumer of the printed ambient
two-output condition by C03's existing specialization. This may be a small
theorem package rather than a new bundled structure. The general necessity
direction must retain its existing generality; the combined finite wrapper
does not replace or weaken C03's theorem.

### 3.3 Conditional entropy bridge

For `[Fintype beta]`, arbitrary `p : PMF (Fin 2)` and
`q : PMF (Fin 2 → beta)`, let `t = canonicalJoint p q` and
`g = q.map (finTwoArrowEquiv beta)`. Establish

`condEntropyOf t Y Z = (mismatchMass g).toReal * entropy p`.

No channel, coupling-marginal condition, singularity, full support of p or
full support of q is needed for this identity. On a positive q-fiber w,
the conditional output law is `p.map (fun x => w x)`. Its entropy is zero
when the entries agree, and `entropy p` when they differ, because a
nonconstant map from `Fin 2` is injective. Null q-fibers contribute zero.

Connect this triple-law identity to a fixed-pair canonical representation on
an unrestricted source through C02 full-law equality and the pinned
pushforward definitions. The corresponding mutual-information formula is

`mutualInfoOf t Y Z = entropyOf t Y - (mismatchMass g).toReal * entropy p`.

Generic conditioning, entropy invariance and information identities must be
reused, not reproved as a local alternative library.

### 3.4 Universal bound and finite information attainment

For `[Fintype beta]`, `p : PMF (Fin 2)` and
`kappa : Fin 2 → PMF beta`, write `pXY = PMF.channelJoint p kappa`.
For any unrestricted source `omega`, `mu : PMF omega`, finite auxiliary
alphabet gamma (in any universe), variables `X : omega → Fin 2`,
`Y : omega → beta`, `Z : omega → gamma`, require exactly

- `jointLaw mu X Y = pXY`; and
- `IsFunctionalRepresentationOf mu X Y Z`.

Then prove

`mutualInfoOf mu Y Z ≤ entropy (sndMarginal pXY) - r(kappa) * entropy p`.

No full-input-support premise is required for this universal bound.
In the full-support branch C02 canonicalization gives all row marginals;
otherwise a binary PMF is a point mass, `entropy p = 0`, and the ordinary
information-versus-output-entropy bound applies. This branch must not infer
the unsupported channel row from the original decoder.

Specify the finite witness

`g* = maximalCoupling (kappa 0) (kappa 1)`,
`q* = g*.map (finTwoArrowEquiv beta).symm`,
`t* = canonicalJoint p q*`.

Prove its canonical/functional-representation property, exact pair marginal
`pXY`, and information equality

`mutualInfoOf t* Y Z = entropy (sndMarginal pXY) - r(kappa) * entropy p`.

The equality must follow through the conditional-entropy bridge; the bare
fact that g* minimizes mismatch is not information-theoretic attainment.
State a finite existential attaining representation and the universal bound
as one usable maximum package (or directly composable theorems). The concrete
finite type `Fin 2 → beta` suffices; a `Fin n` reindexing is needed only for
a consumer requiring C01's existential pair predicate, using its existing
finite-witness bridge. No optimizer structure, compactness, numerical solver,
minimum auxiliary-cardinality claim or infinite-auxiliary theorem is promised.

### 3.5 Equality and support boundary

For the same arbitrary p and kappa, prove

`mutualInfo pXY ≤ r(kappa) * entropy p`.

Singularity implies equality for every prior. Under the additional, explicit
premise `hp : ∀ x, x ∈ p.support`, prove precisely

`r(kappa) * entropy p = mutualInfo pXY ↔ IsSingularChannel kappa`.

Derive this using information attainment, the universal bound, C01's perfect
representation criterion, C02's full-support pair/channel equivalence, and
the completed binary-input Lemma 2. No new strict-concavity, KL-divergence or
equality-case information theory is required. Full support belongs to the
converse recovering the **ambient intrinsic channel**, not to the forward
implication, construction or optimum formula. No division by `entropy p`
is allowed without proving nonzero entropy; the proposed route needs none.

For semantic input transport, take `[Fintype alpha] [Fintype beta]`,
`e : alpha ≃ Fin 2`, `p : PMF alpha` and `kappa : alpha → PMF beta`.
Use `p_e = p.map e`, `kappa_e i = kappa (e.symm i)` and the existing notation
`r_e(kappa)` from Section 3.1. Check explicitly that

`(PMF.channelJoint p kappa).map (fun t => (e t.1, t.2)) =
 PMF.channelJoint p_e kappa_e`.

Transport the input entropy, output marginal, mutual information and full
input support through these laws. For a vector-law witness, the inverse
coordinate transport sends `w : Fin 2 → beta` to `fun x => w (e x)`.
The intrinsic wrapper uses `Fintype.card alpha = 2`; quantitative statements
may retain e with S01's enumeration-independence theorem.

retains its independent cardinality alternatives from Section 3.2. These
